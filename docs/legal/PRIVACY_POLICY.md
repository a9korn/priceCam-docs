# Privacy Policy for PriceCam

**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15

Welcome to PriceCam. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information about you when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

PriceCam is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect any personal information, financial data, or images from you. All processing of images and text recognition is done entirely on your device.

- **Camera and Image Data**: Our app requires access to your device's camera to function. The images you capture and the text recognized from them are processed locally on your device and are never transmitted, stored, or collected by us.
- **Usage Data**: We do not collect any analytics or usage data. We have no way of knowing who you are or how you use the app.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect any of your information, we do not use it for any purpose. All data required for the app's functionality remains on your device.

## 3. Information Sharing

We do not have any of your personal information to share. We do not share data with any third parties.

## 4. Data Security

All data processed by PriceCam is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data. You are in control of your data.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make any material changes, we will notify you by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact me.
